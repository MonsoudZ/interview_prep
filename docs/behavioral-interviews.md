# Behavioral interview practice

Open **Story bank** from Practice to organize examples from your own experience. Tag each example for ownership, incidents, conflict, mentoring or tradeoffs. Each has situation/stakes, your responsibility, specific actions, outcomes/evidence, reflection/learning, and an optional two-minute opening. “Ready to rehearse” is your own assessment; it requires the five main sections. Drafts can be incomplete.

Stories autosave with optimistic versions. A stale tab preserves its local text and offers export before an explicit reload. Search includes section text and topic tags. Export creates a plain-text copy. Archive is reversible and prevents editing/rehearsing until restored; saved sessions retain their copies. Each account can keep 100 examples including archives. Story fields are filtered from Rails request logs, but are stored in the application database, so use anonymized examples and protect backups.

## Rehearsal

Choose one of five senior questions and open a guided rehearsal. This captures the saved story version in `preparation.md`, alongside a fresh `answer.md`. The session records story identity/version and the capture time. Later edits or archives never replace that preparation. A retry of the same request returns the original session. The existing limit of ten unfinished sessions also applies.

Timed practice from Exercises starts with a blank response outline and no imported story. Answers lock until Start; each standalone question has eight minutes enforced by the server. The native writing editor supports search, word counts, export and numbered source lines. You may speak aloud while practicing, but the app records and evaluates no audio. Only the typed response is preserved as the rehearsed answer.

Submission freezes the exact answer and preparation revision. Four senior-level criteria cover clarity, personal contribution, theme-specific judgment, and outcomes/reflection. Self-ratings require explanations and references to 1–20 existing answer lines. Preparation notes cannot be cited as response evidence. Hints, reworks, comparisons, focused drills and scripted follow-ups use the existing saved-session workflows. Behavioral sessions do not enter the code runner or model-review queue. No truthfulness, personality, hiring outcome or calibrated score is inferred.

## Full interview

The **Senior behavioral interview** plan combines all five questions (eight minutes each) and a fourteen-minute scripted follow-up round: 54 minutes total. The continuous server deadline starts with the first stage. Follow-ups capture seven questions across all five submitted sources, including a reflection/transfer prompt. Unanswered stages remain unattempted. The consolidated review/export preserves answer revision provenance and explicit self-assessment.

Current boundaries: this is structured typed practice, not a conversational interviewer, audio/video recorder, or calibrated behavioral assessor. Questions and rubrics are editorial practice material; no employer endorsement or hiring equivalence is claimed.
